Privacy Policy for Dumpix

Last updated: February 2026

Dumpix is a mobile application that allows users to manually browse, review, and delete photos stored on their device.

1. Photo Access

Dumpix requests access to photos stored on your device only to display them for manual review and deletion. All photo access and processing occurs locally on the user's device.

Dumpix does not upload, store, transmit, scan, analyze, or share your photos. No photo content leaves the user's device.

2. Data Collection

Dumpix does not collect or store personal data on its own servers. The application does not require account creation and does not transmit user-generated photo content to external servers.

However, third-party advertising services used by Dumpix (Google AdMob) may collect certain device and advertising-related information as described below.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dumpix uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information for advertising, measurement, and analytics purposes, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • Basic device information (such as device model and OS version)
  • Ad interaction and performance data (such as ad views and clicks)

Dumpix does not receive, store, or control this data. This collection is managed by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Security

Dumpix does not store personal data on external servers. Photo access remains local to the device.

5. User Control

Users may grant, deny, or revoke photo permissions at any time through device settings.

Users may also reset or limit the Advertising ID through device privacy settings (availability depends on the device and Android version).

6. Children's Privacy

Dumpix is not directed to children under the age of 13 and is not intended for use by children under 13. Dumpix does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
